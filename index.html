How to trace

Touch the green dot with your index fingertip to start drawing.

Follow the faded outline. Covered parts turn green.

Drop your hand out of view to lift the pen.

Aim assist pulls your pen onto the line when you're close.

Only your pointing index finger draws. Other hands and curled fingers are ignored.

Strike a Shape

A faded shape appears over your camera view and the clock starts. Trace it in the air with your index finger. Faster, cleaner lines score more.
